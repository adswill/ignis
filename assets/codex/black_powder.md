# Black powder
*Saltpeter + charcoal + sulfur (75 : 15 : 10)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Simplified reaction | 10 KNO₃ + 3 S + 8 C → 2 K₂CO₃ + 3 K₂SO₄ + 6 CO₂ + 5 N₂ |
| Heat | ≈2.7-3 MJ/kg |
| Gas | ≈0.27 m³/kg (≈45 % of mass) |
| Invented | China, ≈9th century |

## In the real world
The oldest explosive. Saltpeter supplies oxygen, charcoal is the main fuel, sulfur lowers the ignition temperature and speeds the burn. The finer and more intimately mixed (traditionally ball-milled and pressed wet), the faster it burns.

## In Ignis
- Put [[saltpeter]] next to [[charcoal]] (+ [[sulfur]]): the pair reacts at **≈600 K** with a **≈2200 K** flame and white smoke.
- Or use premixed [[gunpowder]].

## Try this
Mix the three powders in different proportions and compare how fast each pile burns.
