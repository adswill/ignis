# Permanganate + glycerin
*14 KMnO₄ + 4 C₃H₅(OH)₃ → … + heat (self-igniting)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Delay | ≈5-60 s depending on grain size and temperature |
| Flame | violet-pink from potassium |

## In the real world
Glycerin's hydroxyl groups are oxidised by permanganate. The reaction speeds up as it heats itself, until it ignites with no spark. Cold weather slows it a lot: Arrhenius kinetics in action.

## In Ignis
- [[potassium_permanganate]] beside [[glycerin]] reacts slowly at room temperature, heats itself and bursts into flame.

## Try this
Set up two dishes, one at room temperature and one chilled with [[ice]] around it. Which ignites first?
