# Glass
*A frozen liquid: rigid, transparent, and touchy about sudden temperature changes.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Soda-lime composition | ≈73 % SiO₂, 14 % Na₂O, 9 % CaO |
| Density | ≈2500 kg/m³ |
| Glass transition | ≈560 °C |
| Softening point | ≈720 °C |
| Thermal expansion | ≈9 ppm/K (soda-lime) vs ≈3.3 ppm/K (borosilicate) |

## In the real world
Glass is an amorphous solid: its atoms are frozen in a disordered liquid-like arrangement. Ordinary window glass cracks if one part is heated much more than another (thermal shock), which is why lab and oven glassware is borosilicate, with a third of the thermal expansion.

**Uses:** Windows, containers, optical fibres, lenses, insulation (fibreglass).

**Hazards:** Thermal shock fractures; sharp fragments.

## In Ignis
- Melts above **≈1440 K** into [[molten_glass]].
- Made by melting [[sand]].

## Try this
Melt a sand pile into glass with the torch and pour [[water]] on it while it cools.
