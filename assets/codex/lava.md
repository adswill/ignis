# Lava
*Molten rock at 1100-1200 °C, flowing out of the Earth.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Basalt lava temperature | ≈1100-1200 °C |
| Rhyolite lava temperature | ≈700-850 °C |
| Density | ≈2600-2800 kg/m³ |
| Viscosity | ≈10-1000 Pa·s (basalt); far higher for rhyolite |

## In the real world
Lava is magma that reached the surface. Runny basaltic lava (Hawaii, Iceland) forms smooth pāhoehoe and rubbly ʻaʻā; sticky silica-rich lavas trap gas and erupt explosively. Where lava meets the sea, littoral steam explosions throw out glass and rock.

**Uses:** Geothermal energy; basalt fibres; the land of Hawaii and Iceland.

**Hazards:** Steam explosions where it meets water; toxic gases (SO₂, laze: hydrochloric acid steam at sea entries).

## In Ignis
- Starts at **≈1290 K**; freezes into [[stone]] below **≈1080 K**.
- Sets fire to burnables and boils [[water]] explosively.

## Recipes
- [[stone]] heated above ≈1230 K → lava.
- Lava + [[water]] → [[steam_explosion]] + [[stone]].

## Try this
Pour lava down a slope into a lake.
