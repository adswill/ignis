# Rusting
*4 Fe + 3 O₂ + 2 n H₂O → 2 Fe₂O₃·nH₂O*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat | ≈7.4 MJ per kg of iron (to Fe₂O₃): the same as burning it, just slowly |
| Salt | speeds rusting several-fold |

## In the real world
Rusting is slow combustion: iron oxidising releases the same total energy as burning it, but over years. Hand warmers speed it up with salt water, activated carbon and fine iron powder to give hours of heat.

## In Ignis
- [[iron_filings]] next to [[water]] slowly turn into [[rust]]; [[brine]] makes it much faster.
