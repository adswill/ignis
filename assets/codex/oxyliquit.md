# Oxyliquit
*Liquid oxygen soaked into charcoal*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Used | mining, ≈1900-1950 (Germany, Chile) |
| Shelf life | minutes: the LOX boils off |

## In the real world
A cartridge of carbon soaked in liquid oxygen detonates powerfully, and after a few minutes the oxygen boils off and it's harmless. Its unpredictability ended its use; it remains a vivid lesson in LOX safety.

## In Ignis
- [[liquid_oxygen]] beside any fuel reacts violently (ignition ≈300 K, ≈3300 K).

## Try this
Pour LOX onto charcoal and add a tiny spark.
