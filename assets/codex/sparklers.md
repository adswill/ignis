# Sparklers
*Oxidiser + iron filings: a slow burn that throws sparks*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Burn temperature | ≈1000-1600 °C |
| Spark colour | gold from iron, white from aluminium/magnesium |

## In the real world
A sparkler is an oxidiser-fuel paste on a wire with iron or steel filings mixed in. The filings heat up, fly out and burn in the air as branching sparks: the sparks fork as carbon in the steel bursts the droplet.

## In Ignis
- [[saltpeter]] beside [[iron_filings]] reacts slower and cooler but the iron burns to [[rust]].

## Try this
Make a line of saltpeter with iron filings mixed in and light it.
