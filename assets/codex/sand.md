# Sand
*Ground-up quartz: inert, heavy, and the raw material of glass.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Mineral | mostly quartz, SiO₂ |
| Grain density | 2650 kg/m³ |
| Bulk density | ≈1500-1700 kg/m³ |
| Melting point (quartz) | ≈1710 °C (≈1980 K) |
| Angle of repose | ≈30-35° dry |

## In the real world
Sand is rock broken into 0.06-2 mm grains, usually quartz because quartz is hard and chemically stubborn. It does not burn and absorbs heat well, so it smothers fires by cutting off air. Melting it with soda ash and lime makes window [[glass]].

**Uses:** Concrete, glassmaking, casting moulds for molten metal, sandbags and blast tamping, fire buckets.

**Hazards:** Fine crystalline silica dust causes silicosis when inhaled over years.

## In Ignis
- Falls and piles into cones like a real granular material.
- Inert: buries and smothers flames by keeping oxygen away.
- Melts at **≈1710 K** into [[molten_glass]]: pour [[molten_metal]] or [[lava]] onto sand and make glass.

## Recipes
- Sand + intense heat → [[molten_glass]] → [[glass]].

## Try this
Light a [[kerosene]] pool fire, then pour a sand heap on top. Watch the flame starve. Then dump [[thermite]] on sand and look for glass.
