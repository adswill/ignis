# Catalysis
*A shortcut for a reaction that doesn't get used up.*

A catalyst offers a different reaction path with a lower activation energy, so the reaction runs much faster at the same temperature, and the catalyst comes out unchanged. Car catalytic converters (platinum, palladium, rhodium) burn leftover fuel and CO at a few hundred degrees. Manganese dioxide makes [[hydrogen_peroxide]] fall apart; iron oxide speeds up solid propellant burning; enzymes are the catalysts of life.
