# Dust explosions
*Anything that burns as a lump explodes as a cloud.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Dust explosion pentagon | fuel, oxygen, ignition, dispersion, confinement |
| Minimum explosible concentration | ≈30-100 g/m³ for many dusts |
| Particle size | typically < 0.5 mm; finer is worse |
| Famous events | Washburn A Mill 1878; Courrières 1906; Imperial Sugar 2008 |

A heap of combustible powder burns slowly because air reaches only its outer surface. Suspended in air, each grain is surrounded by oxygen and the total surface area is enormous, so a flame can race through the cloud like a gas explosion. Inside a building, the first explosion often shakes settled dust off beams into a second, much larger cloud: the **secondary explosion** usually does the most damage. Housekeeping (cleaning up dust) is the main prevention.

## In Ignis
- Fuel powders with lots of exposed surface (falling or blown into the air) burn several times faster than a settled pile.

## Try this
Blow [[flour]] around a closed box with fans and fire the igniter. Then try the same amount in a heap.
