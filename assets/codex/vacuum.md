# Vacuum
*Sucks gas out.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Low Earth orbit | ≈10⁻⁷-10⁻⁹ atm |
| Best lab vacuum | ≈10⁻¹³ atm and below |

## In the real world
Pull the gas out and there is nothing to burn and nothing to carry sound. Rapid decompression drops pressure and temperature together.

## In Ignis
- Removes gas where you paint: a sudden low pressure that pulls surrounding gas in.

## Try this
Vacuum the inside of a closed box and watch the walls get stressed inward... and the inrush when you open it.
