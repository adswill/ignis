# Vent
*An opening to the outside world at 1 atm and 300 K.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Burst disc | a deliberate weak point sized to fail before the vessel |

## In the real world
Every pressure vessel needs a way to get rid of excess pressure. Rupture discs and relief valves are sized so the vent can release gas faster than the worst-case fire or reaction can generate it. Explosion vents on dust silos are panels designed to blow out first.

## In Ignis
- Holds the gas at ambient air (**1 atm, 300 K**): exhaust leaves through vents, fresh air enters through them.

## Try this
Build two boxes with the same fuel charge: one sealed, one with a 2-cell vent. Light both.
