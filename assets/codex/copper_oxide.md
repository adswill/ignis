# Copper oxide
*Black copper oxide: the oxidiser in the most violent thermite.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | CuO |
| Density | 6310 kg/m³ |
| Melting point | 1326 °C |
| Copper thermite | 3 CuO + 2 Al → 3 Cu + Al₂O₃, ≈4 MJ/kg of mix |

## In the real world
Copper gives up oxygen more readily than iron, so copper thermite reacts much faster than iron thermite. The temperature reached is above copper's boiling point (2562 °C), so part of the copper vaporises and the mix goes off with a bang rather than a pour.

**Uses:** Pigment for blue/green glass and ceramics, copper thermite welding of electrical cables, catalysts.

**Hazards:** Copper thermite can be explosively fast.

## In Ignis
- Inert alone; with metal powders it makes [[copper_thermite]] (ignites ≈1140 K, reacts very fast).

## Recipes
- Copper oxide + [[aluminium_powder]] or [[magnesium]] → [[copper_thermite]].

## Try this
Light iron thermite and copper thermite side by side. One pours molten metal, the other blasts.
