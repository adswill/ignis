# Sodium
*A soft metal you can cut with a knife that explodes in water.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | 968 kg/m³ (floats on water) |
| Melting / boiling point | 97.8 °C / 883 °C |
| Reaction | 2 Na + 2 H₂O → 2 NaOH + H₂, ≈184 kJ per mol Na |
| Flame colour | intense yellow (589 nm) |

## In the real world
Sodium gives up its outer electron so easily that it tears water apart, making hydrogen gas and sodium hydroxide. The heat melts the sodium into a skittering ball and often lights the hydrogen. Larger lumps explode: the metal's surface explodes outwards as charge builds up (a 'Coulomb explosion', filmed in 2015). It is stored under oil.

**Uses:** Sodium-vapour street lamps (the yellow light), coolant in some fast nuclear reactors, chemical synthesis.

**Hazards:** Reacts violently with water and moisture; burning sodium cannot be put out with water.

## In Ignis
- Reacts with neighbouring [[water]]: releases hydrogen, heats up and turns the water into [[lye]].
- Burns in air above **≈480 K**.

## Recipes
- Sodium + [[water]] → hydrogen + [[lye]] + heat ([[alkali_water]]).

## Try this
Drop a few sodium grains into a water tank and light the gas above it with the torch. Then try [[potassium]].
