# Nitroglycerin
*An oily liquid explosive so sensitive a bump can set it off.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | C₃H₅(ONO₂)₃ |
| Density | 1600 kg/m³ |
| Freezing point | 13 °C |
| Detonation velocity | ≈7700 m/s |
| Heat of explosion | ≈6.3 MJ/kg |
| Discovered | Ascanio Sobrero, 1847 |

## In the real world
Nitroglycerin carries more than enough oxygen to burn all its own carbon and hydrogen, and it releases it in microseconds when shocked. Transporting it killed many, including Alfred Nobel's brother Emil in 1864. Nobel tamed it by soaking it into diatomaceous earth: dynamite. In tiny doses it is a heart medicine that widens blood vessels.

**Uses:** Dynamite, double-base smokeless powder and rocket propellant, angina treatment.

**Hazards:** Shock-, friction- and heat-sensitive. Causes severe headaches on skin contact.

## In Ignis
- Detonates above **≈490 K**, under a **≈3 atm** shock, or when touched by hot gas (≈1500 K).
- Flows and pools like any liquid. Absorb it into something to make it safer: [[dynamite]].

## Try this
Put a drop of nitroglycerin on a steel plate and drop [[sand]] on it from above. Then hit it with a small pressure wave from [[gunpowder]].
