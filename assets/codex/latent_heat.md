# Latent heat & phase change
*Energy that changes state instead of temperature.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Water: fusion / vaporisation | 334 / 2257 kJ/kg |
| Ethanol vaporisation | 841 kJ/kg |
| Liquid nitrogen vaporisation | 199 kJ/kg |
| Liquid oxygen vaporisation | 213 kJ/kg |
| Dry ice sublimation | 571 kJ/kg |
| Iron fusion | 247 kJ/kg |

Melting ice stays at 0 °C until it has all melted, and boiling water stays at 100 °C until it has all boiled: the heat goes into breaking the bonds between molecules rather than raising the temperature. That 'hidden' heat is **latent heat**. It's why sweat cools you, why ice packs last, and why water is such a good extinguisher.

## In Ignis
- Melting, freezing and boiling all hold the cell at the transition temperature while the latent heat is absorbed or released.

## Try this
Watch an ice block heated by a flame in the **Heat** view: it sits at 273 K until it's all water.
