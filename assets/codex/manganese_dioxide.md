# Manganese dioxide
*A black powder that makes hydrogen peroxide fall apart into oxygen.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | MnO₂ |
| Density | ≈5030 kg/m³ |
| Decomposes | above ≈535 °C to Mn₂O₃ + O₂ |
| Role | catalyst: speeds a reaction without being used up |

## In the real world
MnO₂ provides surfaces where peroxide molecules break apart much faster, so a spoonful in concentrated peroxide makes a jet of steam and oxygen. It is also the cathode material in ordinary alkaline batteries.

**Uses:** Alkaline and zinc-carbon batteries, glass decolouriser, catalyst in chemistry labs and some rocket gas generators.

**Hazards:** Harmful dust; strong oxidiser in contact with fuels.

## In Ignis
- Inert on its own; a strong catalyst for [[hydrogen_peroxide]] decomposition.

## Recipes
- [[hydrogen_peroxide]] + MnO₂ → oxygen + steam ([[peroxide_decomposition]]).

## Try this
Put a pinch of MnO₂ at the bottom of a peroxide tank. Then put a flame above it: the oxygen makes it roar.
