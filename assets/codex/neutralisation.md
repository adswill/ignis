# Neutralisation
*HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O + 57 kJ/mol*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat of neutralisation | ≈-57 kJ per mol of water formed (strong acid + strong base) |

## In the real world
Acids donate H⁺, bases supply OH⁻, and the two combine into water. The leftover ions form a salt. The heat released is the same for any strong acid and strong base, because the reaction is always H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O.
