# Air inlet
*Feeds compressed air at the air feed pressure.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Air needed for methane | ≈17.2 kg of air per kg of methane (stoichiometric) |

## In the real world
Air-breathing engines take oxygen from the atmosphere, so they carry only fuel. Jet engines run overall very lean (lots of excess air) to keep turbine temperatures survivable: only part of the air is burned with the fuel; the rest cools and dilutes the hot gas.

## In Ignis
- Held at **p_air** with [[air]] (21 % oxygen, 79 % nitrogen).

## Try this
Feed a combustor with only air and fuel, then add a dilution-air row downstream and watch the exit temperature fall.
