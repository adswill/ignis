# Air
*The atmosphere: 78 % nitrogen, 21 % oxygen, 1 % argon and a pinch of CO₂.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | 1.225 kg/m³ at sea level, 15 °C |
| Pressure | 101.325 kPa = 1 atm |
| Speed of sound | 343 m/s at 20 °C |
| Heat capacity ratio γ | 1.40 |
| Composition | 78.08 % N₂, 20.95 % O₂, 0.93 % Ar, ≈0.04 % CO₂ |

## In the real world
Every fire on Earth breathes air. Its oxygen content is the reason flames in air top out near 2200 K, and its pressure sets how much thrust a rocket nozzle loses at sea level. Hot air rises because it is less dense than the cold air around it.

**Uses:** Everything.

**Hazards:** Compressed air stores a lot of energy: a burst tank is a pressure explosion.
