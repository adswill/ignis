# Peroxide decomposition
*2 H₂O₂ → 2 H₂O + O₂ + heat*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat | -98 kJ per mol H₂O₂ (≈2.9 MJ/kg of pure peroxide) |
| 90 % HTP products | ≈42 % O₂ + 58 % steam by mass, ≈1020 K |
| Specific impulse (monopropellant) | ≈160 s |

## In the real world
Catalysts like manganese dioxide, permanganate or silver mesh rip peroxide apart. Concentrated peroxide decomposes into a jet of superheated steam and oxygen: a monopropellant rocket with no flame. Elephant toothpaste is the same reaction with soap to catch the oxygen.

## In Ignis
- [[hydrogen_peroxide]] beside [[manganese_dioxide]], [[potassium_permanganate]], [[rust]] or iron decomposes into hot oxygen + steam.
- Hot peroxide (> **≈420 K**) runs away on its own.

## Try this
Feed the oxygen from a peroxide tank into a fuel spray. Instant afterburner.
