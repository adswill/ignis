# Hydrochloric acid
*A strong acid that dissolves metals into hydrogen and salts.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | HCl (aq) |
| Concentrated | ≈37 % HCl, density ≈1180 kg/m³ |
| pH | below 1 |
| Stomach acid | ≈0.5 % HCl, pH ≈1.5-3.5 |
| Fumes | concentrated acid gives off HCl gas |

## In the real world
Hydrochloric acid is hydrogen chloride gas dissolved in water. Reactive metals like magnesium, zinc and aluminium dissolve in it, releasing hydrogen; iron dissolves slowly; rust dissolves without gas. Your stomach makes a dilute version to digest food.

**Uses:** Steel pickling (removing rust), pH control, PVC production, cleaning masonry.

**Hazards:** Corrosive to skin and eyes; fumes damage lungs.

## In Ignis
- Evaporates slowly into fumes and boils at **≈360 K**.
- Attacks [[magnesium]] (fast), [[aluminium_powder]], [[iron_filings]] (slow): hydrogen + heat; dissolves [[rust]] and [[copper_oxide]].
- Neutralised by [[lye]].

## Recipes
- HCl + metal → hydrogen + salt solution ([[acid_metal]]).
- HCl + [[lye]] → [[brine]] + heat ([[neutralisation]]).

## Try this
Put magnesium, aluminium and iron powders in three acid pools. Compare the fizzing.
