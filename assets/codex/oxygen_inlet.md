# Oxygen inlet
*Feeds pure oxygen at the oxygen feed pressure.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Methane rocket O/F ratio | ≈3.6 (Raptor), stoichiometric ≈4.0 |
| Kerosene rocket O/F | ≈2.3-2.7 |

## In the real world
Rockets carry their oxygen because there is none in space. Engines run slightly fuel-rich of stoichiometric because the lighter exhaust molecules give better specific impulse and the cooler gas is kinder to the hardware.

## In Ignis
- Held at **p_o2** with pure [[oxygen]].
- With a fuel inlet and a nozzle you have a liquid rocket.
