# Fan
*Pushes gas in the direction of its arrow.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Turbofan mass flow | ≈1 t/s of air at take-off for a big engine (e.g. GE90) |
| Bypass ratio | ≈9-12 in modern airliner engines |
| Compressor pressure ratio | ≈40-60 overall in modern turbofans |

## In the real world
Fans and compressors add momentum and pressure to gas. A jet engine's compressor squeezes air dozens of times before fuel is burned in it; the hot gas then drives a turbine that powers the compressor. In fire science, forced air (a bellows, a forge blower) makes fuel burn much hotter because oxygen arrives faster.

## In Ignis
- Pushes gas along its arrow (**R** rotates). Fan power is a global slider.
- Stack columns of fans to make a compressor.
- Blow on a fire to feed it oxygen, or lift dust into a cloud.
