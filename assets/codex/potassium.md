# Potassium
*Like sodium, only angrier.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | 862 kg/m³ |
| Melting / boiling point | 63.5 °C / 759 °C |
| Reaction | 2 K + 2 H₂O → 2 KOH + H₂ |
| Flame colour | lilac |

## In the real world
Potassium sits below sodium in the periodic table: its outer electron is further from the nucleus and even easier to remove, so it reacts with water faster and usually ignites the hydrogen it makes with a lilac flame. Essential in our bodies as ions, deadly as metal.

**Uses:** Mostly as compounds: potash fertiliser, [[saltpeter]], soap.

**Hazards:** Can form explosive peroxides in storage; reacts violently with water.

## In Ignis
- Reacts with [[water]] even harder than [[sodium]], and shatters violently: small explosions.
- Ignites in air above **≈420 K**.

## Recipes
- Potassium + [[water]] → hydrogen + [[lye]] + bangs ([[alkali_water]]).

Drop sodium and potassium into two identical water tanks side by side.
