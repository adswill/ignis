# Torch
*Your hand-held flame: heats gas to 2700 K and solid surfaces it touches.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Oxy-acetylene flame | ≈3500 K, hottest common fuel gas flame |
| Propane torch in air | ≈2200 K |
| Match head | ≈1100-1700 K |

## In the real world
Torches burn a fuel gas with air or oxygen in a stabilised jet. Oxy-acetylene is hot enough to melt steel; cutting torches add a jet of pure oxygen that burns the hot steel itself.

## In Ignis
- Heats gas to **≈2700 K** and the surface of solids: lights any burnable mixture, wood, propellant or explosive it touches.

## Try this
Hold the torch on a [[steel]] wall and watch it heat; then on [[copper]], which spreads the heat away.
