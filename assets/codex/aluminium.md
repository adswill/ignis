# Aluminium
*One third the weight of steel, and one third the heat tolerance.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | 2700 kg/m³ |
| Melting point | 660.3 °C (933 K) |
| Thermal conductivity | 237 W/(m·K) pure; ≈167 for alloy 6061 |
| Specific heat | 897 J/(kg·K) |
| Yield strength | ≈275 MPa (6061-T6) |
| Strength at 200 °C | roughly half of room-temperature |

## In the real world
Aluminium is the most abundant metal in Earth's crust, but it binds so tightly to oxygen that it was more precious than gold until the Hall-Héroult electrolysis process (1886). A 4 nm oxide skin protects it from corrosion. Alloys are strong for their weight but soften quickly with heat, so hot engine parts are rarely aluminium.

**Uses:** Airliner skins, drink cans, heat sinks, rocket propellant tanks (Falcon 9 tanks are aluminium-lithium).

**Hazards:** As a fine powder it is a powerful fuel: see [[aluminium_powder]].

## In Ignis
- Weak (strength **2.0**) and the first metal to give up: it softens near room temperature in game units and melts at **≈660 K**.
- Spreads heat fast (conductivity 2.6× steel), so it smooths hot spots before it fails.
- Melts into [[molten_metal]] that freezes back into aluminium.
