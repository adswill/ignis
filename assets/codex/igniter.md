# Igniter
*A glow plug that heats nearby gas to about 3000 K while igniters are on.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Spark plug spark | ≈0.1-1 mJ is plenty for methane-air (minimum ignition energy ≈0.28 mJ) |
| Glow plug | ≈1000 °C surface |

## In the real world
To start a flame you only need to heat a small pocket of mixture above its ignition temperature; the reaction heat then spreads itself. Jet engines and rocket engines use igniters only during start-up; afterwards the flame holds itself.

## In Ignis
- Heats neighbouring gas to **≈3000 K** and touching solids while igniters are switched on (**I**).
- Lights wood, propellant and explosives that touch it.

## Try this
Fill a box with a lean methane mix (barely above 5 %) and test whether the igniter can light it.
