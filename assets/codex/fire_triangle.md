# Fire triangle
*Fuel, oxygen, heat: take away any one and the fire dies.*

## The idea
A fire needs **fuel**, an **oxidiser** (usually oxygen in air) and enough **heat** to keep the reaction going. Fire scientists add a fourth side, the **chain reaction**: flames are kept alive by free radicals (H, OH, O), and some extinguishing agents work by mopping those up.

## How each extinguisher works
- **Water**: removes heat (its [[latent_heat]] is huge) and the steam dilutes oxygen.
- **CO₂ and inert gases**: dilute oxygen below the limit (methane needs roughly 12 % O₂ or more).
- **Foam and blankets, sand**: cut fuel off from oxygen.
- **Dry chemical powders, halons**: break the chain reaction.
- **Cutting the fuel**: closing a gas valve, firebreaks in wildfires.

## In Ignis
- All four sides are simulated: starve a fire of fuel or oxygen, chill it, or bury it in [[sand]].

## Try this
Start four identical fires. Put one out with water, one with sand, one with the chill tool, one by sealing it in a box.
