# Plastic (polyethylene)
*Solid oil. Melts, drips, and burns with thick black smoke.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | ≈910-970 kg/m³ (floats on water) |
| Melting point | ≈105-135 °C (LDPE-HDPE) |
| Autoignition | ≈350 °C |
| Heat of combustion | ≈46 MJ/kg (same as fuel oil) |
| World production | ≈100 million tonnes/year of polyethylene |

## In the real world
Polyethylene is long chains of CH₂ units: chemically it is close to wax, and it stores as much energy as diesel. In fires it melts and breaks down into hydrocarbon vapours before it burns, so plastic fires spread by burning drips and produce dense soot.

**Uses:** Packaging, bottles, pipes, cable insulation.

**Hazards:** Burning drips; smoke contains CO and other toxic compounds.

## In Ignis
- Melts at **≈435 K** into [[molten_plastic]], which boils into fuel vapour at **≈690 K**: the vapour burns with heavy smoke.

## Try this
Hang a plastic block above a small flame and watch it drip, then flare up.
