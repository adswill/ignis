# Sugar
*Sucrose: food, fuel, and the classic amateur rocket propellant.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ |
| Density | 1587 kg/m³ |
| Melts / decomposes | ≈186 °C (caramelises) |
| Heat of combustion | 16.5 MJ/kg |
| Dust explosion | Imperial Sugar refinery, Georgia, 2008: 14 dead |

## In the real world
Sugar is a carbohydrate packed with chemical energy, which is exactly why our bodies run on it. Heated, it melts and caramelises, then chars. As a fine dust suspended in air it can explode: the 2008 Imperial Sugar refinery blast was a sugar-dust explosion.

**Uses:** Food, fermentation to ethanol, rocket candy.

**Hazards:** Sugar dust clouds are explosive; molten sugar causes severe burns because it sticks.

## In Ignis
- Burns in air above **≈540 K** at about **1500 K**.
- A fuel for [[saltpeter]] and [[ammonium_perchlorate]].

## Recipes
- [[saltpeter]] + sugar (65:35) → [[rocket_candy]].
- [[ammonium_perchlorate]] + sugar → a fast [[apcp_mix]].

## Try this
Blow sugar into the air with a [[fan]] and light it: a dust cloud burns far faster than a pile. See [[dust_explosions]].
