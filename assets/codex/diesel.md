# Diesel
*A heavy fuel that's hard to light with a match but easy to light by squeezing.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | ≈820-850 kg/m³ |
| Boiling range | ≈180-360 °C |
| Flash point | ≥52 °C |
| Autoignition | ≈210 °C |
| Lower heating value | ≈42.6 MJ/kg |

## In the real world
Diesel has a high flash point, so a match dropped in a bucket usually goes out. But its autoignition temperature is low, which is exactly what diesel engines exploit: air compressed about 15-20:1 gets hot enough (over 500 °C) to ignite the fuel sprayed into it, no spark needed.

**Uses:** Trucks, ships, trains, generators, heating oil, ANFO.

**Hazards:** Fine sprays are much more flammable than pools; burns with heavy soot.

## In Ignis
- Low volatility: boils at **≈540 K**, so you need to heat it before it burns.
- A fuel for [[anfo]].

## Recipes
- Diesel + [[ammonium_nitrate]] → [[anfo]].

## Try this
Try to light a diesel pool with a brief torch flick, then hold the torch on it longer.
