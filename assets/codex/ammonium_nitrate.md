# Ammonium nitrate
*Fertiliser, mining explosive, and the cause of some of the biggest accidental blasts in history.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | NH₄NO₃ |
| Density | 1725 kg/m³ |
| Melting point | 169.6 °C |
| Decomposition | above ≈210 °C |
| ANFO | 94 % AN + 6 % fuel oil; detonation velocity ≈3000-4500 m/s |
| Beirut 2020 | ≈2750 t stored AN; ≈218 dead |

## In the real world
AN is the world's most used nitrogen fertiliser and, mixed with fuel oil, the world's most used commercial explosive (ANFO). It is insensitive: a flame usually makes it melt and smoulder, and it needs a booster charge to detonate. But large heated stockpiles have detonated: Texas City 1947, Toulouse 2001, Tianjin 2015 and Beirut 2020.

**Uses:** Fertiliser, ANFO blasting in mines and quarries, cold packs (dissolving it absorbs heat).

**Hazards:** Contaminated or heated in bulk it can detonate; it releases toxic nitrogen oxides when it burns.

## In Ignis
- Decomposes above **≈480 K**, releasing hot gas.
- With a fuel it makes [[anfo]]: burns poorly, but detonates if a strong shock hits it.

## Recipes
- AN + [[kerosene]] or [[charcoal]] or [[diesel]] → [[anfo]].

## Try this
Light ANFO with the torch: it smoulders. Now put a [[blasting_cap]] in the middle and set it off.
