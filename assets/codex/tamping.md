# Tamping & confinement
*Bury an explosive and more of its energy goes into the target.*

An explosive in the open spends most of its energy pushing air. Covering or packing it (**tamping**, or **stemming** a borehole with clay or crushed rock) holds the gas in for a moment longer, so more of the push goes into the rock or wall. Confinement also changes how deflagrating materials behave: black powder whooshes in the open but bursts its container; ANFO only detonates well when confined in a borehole.

## In Ignis
- Confine charges with walls, [[sand]] or [[stone]] to direct the blast.

## Try this
Set off the same charge on top of a steel plate and buried in sand on top of it. Compare damage.
