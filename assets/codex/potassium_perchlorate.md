# Potassium perchlorate
*A stable, oxygen-rich salt behind fireworks and flash powder.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | KClO₄ |
| Density | 2520 kg/m³ |
| Decomposition | ≈550-610 °C: KClO₄ → KCl + 2 O₂ |
| Oxygen content | 46 % by mass |
| Flash powder | typically 70 % KClO₄ + 30 % aluminium |

## In the real world
Perchlorates hold lots of oxygen but are more stable than the old chlorates, which made them the standard oxidiser of modern fireworks. Mixed with fine metal powder they make flash powder, which explodes even without a container.

**Uses:** Fireworks, flares, airbag initiators, oxygen candles, solid rocket igniters.

**Hazards:** Flash powder mixes are dangerously sensitive to friction and static.

## In Ignis
- Decomposes above **≈660 K** releasing oxygen.
- With metal powders it makes [[flash_powder]] (fastest pair reaction in the game).

## Recipes
- KClO₄ + [[aluminium_powder]] or [[magnesium]] → [[flash_powder]].
- KClO₄ + [[sugar]] or [[charcoal]] → fast burning mix.

## Try this
Light a small mound of flash powder in the open, then the same mound of [[black_powder]]. One fizzes, one bangs.
