# Alkali metals + water
*2 Na + 2 H₂O → 2 NaOH + H₂ + heat*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat | ≈184 kJ per mol of sodium |
| Reactivity order | Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs |

## In the real world
Each alkali metal gives up its single outer electron more easily than the one above it, so the reaction gets more violent going down the group. Hydrogen and heat are produced; the hydrogen often ignites. Big lumps explode by Coulomb repulsion as the surface loses electrons.

## In Ignis
- [[sodium]] or [[potassium]] beside [[water]]: hydrogen, heat, [[lye]]. Hot [[magnesium]] does it too.

## Try this
Drop sodium into a lake with a torch-lit flame hovering above the surface.
