# APCP mix
*Ammonium perchlorate + aluminium powder (or sugar)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Typical composition | ≈70 % AP, ≈16-18 % Al, ≈12-14 % rubber binder |
| Flame temperature | ≈3200-3500 K |
| Main products | Al₂O₃ (smoke), HCl, H₂O, CO, N₂ |

## In the real world
Perchlorate oxidiser, aluminium fuel and a rubbery binder are the basis of nearly every large solid motor. The aluminium burns in droplets behind the surface, raising the flame temperature; the alumina makes the white exhaust plume.

## In Ignis
- [[ammonium_perchlorate]] next to [[aluminium_powder]]: ignites at **≈600 K**, very hot (**≈3000 K+**) and faster at high pressure ([[saint_roberts_law]]).

## Try this
Fill two tubes with the same mix, one with a narrow exit and one wide. The narrow one burns much faster.
