# Flash powder
*Potassium perchlorate + aluminium (70 : 30)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Burn | so fast it explodes unconfined |
| Flame | ≈3000 K+, brilliant white |

## In the real world
Fireworks 'salutes' and stage flashes use flash powder. Fine metal and a strong oxidiser react so fast that even an open pile produces a bang. It is the most dangerous mixture amateur pyrotechnicians handle.

## In Ignis
- [[potassium_perchlorate]] beside a metal powder: the fastest pair reaction in Ignis (rate 25 vs 1.5 for black powder).

## Try this
Light one cell of flash powder. Then light a 10×10 square from a safe distance.
