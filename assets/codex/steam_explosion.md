# Steam explosion
*Water flashing to steam 1700 times its volume, all at once.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Expansion | ≈1600-1700× volume at 1 atm |
| Industrial cause | a leading cause of foundry injuries |
| Grease-fire fireball | a cup of water on burning oil throws a fireball metres high |

## In the real world
When water touches something far above its boiling point, it can be trapped, superheated, and then boil explosively. Under molten metal, lava or burning oil, the expanding steam hurls the hot liquid outward. Not a chemical explosion, but just as dangerous.

## In Ignis
- [[water]] touching [[molten_metal]], [[lava]] or burning oil flashes to steam with a pressure burst.

## Try this
Make a pan of burning [[cooking_oil]] and drip a single cell of water into it.
