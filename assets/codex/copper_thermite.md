# Copper thermite
*3 CuO + 2 Al → 3 Cu + Al₂O₃*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Energy | ≈4.1 MJ/kg of mix |
| Speed | much faster than iron thermite; partly vaporises the copper |

## In the real world
Copper oxide gives up its oxygen more easily, so the reaction is faster and hotter relative to copper's boiling point (2562 °C). Copper vapour expanding makes it go off with a bang. Controlled versions weld copper electrical conductors.

## In Ignis
- [[copper_oxide]] beside [[aluminium_powder]]: ignites at **≈1140 K**, reacts five times faster than iron thermite and makes some hot gas.

## Try this
Light copper thermite in a small closed steel box. Compare with iron thermite.
