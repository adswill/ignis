# Acid + metal
*Mg + 2 HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Activity series | K > Na > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > … > Cu (doesn't react) |

## In the real world
Metals more reactive than hydrogen displace it from acids. Magnesium fizzes violently, aluminium slowly (its oxide skin resists at first), iron slower still. Copper doesn't react with hydrochloric acid at all. Metal oxides like rust dissolve without making gas.

## In Ignis
- [[hydrochloric_acid]] attacks [[magnesium]] fast, [[aluminium_powder]] medium, [[iron_filings]] slowly: hydrogen + a little heat.
- [[rust]] and [[copper_oxide]] dissolve quietly.

## Try this
Fill three acid pools and drop a different metal in each. Light the gas above them.
