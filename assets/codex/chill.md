# Chill
*Pulls heat out of gas.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Liquid nitrogen | 77 K |
| Dry ice | 195 K |

## In the real world
Cooling is the first way of fighting fire: take away enough heat and the reaction can't sustain itself, even with fuel and oxygen present. That's what water and CO₂ extinguishers do.

## In Ignis
- Cools gas where you paint. Use it to quench flames or study ignition.

## Try this
Chill a burning methane cloud. It goes out once the gas falls below ignition temperature.
