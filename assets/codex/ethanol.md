# Ethanol
*Alcohol: burns with a clean, nearly invisible blue flame.*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Formula | C₂H₅OH |
| Density | 789 kg/m³ |
| Boiling point | 78.4 °C (351.5 K) |
| Flash point | 13 °C |
| Autoignition | ≈363 °C |
| Heat of combustion | 26.8 MJ/kg (LHV) |
| Flammability limits | 3.3-19 % in air |

## In the real world
Ethanol already contains some oxygen, so it releases less energy than petrol but burns cleanly with very little soot. The German V-2 burned 75 % ethanol / 25 % water with liquid oxygen: the water lowered the flame temperature so the chamber survived. Ethanol flames can be almost invisible in daylight.

**Uses:** Drinks, fuel additive (E10, E85), solvent, disinfectant, early rockets (V-2, Redstone).

**Hazards:** Invisible flames; vapour is heavier than air and flows to ignition sources.

## In Ignis
- Evaporates readily and boils at **≈351 K**; burns cleanly with little smoke.
