# Rocket candy
*Saltpeter + sugar (65 : 35)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Specific impulse | ≈110-130 s |
| Combustion temperature | ≈1600-1700 K |
| Smoke | heavy white (potassium carbonate) |

## In the real world
Melted or pressed together, potassium nitrate and sugar make a cheap, forgiving solid propellant. Amateur groups have flown it above 10 km. It burns slower and cooler than APCP, with lots of white smoke.

## In Ignis
- [[saltpeter]] beside [[sugar]] reacts like black powder: a steady, smoky burn.
