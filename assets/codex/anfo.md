# ANFO
*Ammonium nitrate + fuel oil (94 : 6)*

## At a glance
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Detonation velocity | ≈3000-4500 m/s (confinement-dependent) |
| Share of US commercial explosives | ≈80 % |
| Energy | ≈3.7 MJ/kg |
| Sensitivity | needs a booster: not cap-sensitive |

## In the real world
ANFO is cheap, safe to handle and pours into boreholes. It is a 'non-ideal' explosive: the reaction zone is wide, so it needs a large diameter and confinement to detonate fully, and a booster charge to start.

## In Ignis
- [[ammonium_nitrate]] beside a fuel ([[kerosene]], [[diesel]], [[charcoal]]): burns poorly when lit, but detonates when a strong shock arrives.

## Try this
Pack ANFO in a stone borehole with a [[blasting_cap]] at the bottom.
